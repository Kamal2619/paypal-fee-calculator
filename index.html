PayPal India Fee Rules, 24h Auto-Hold & Invoice Math
  • Standard Transaction Fee: 4.40% + $0.30 USD per incoming international transaction.
  • Indian GST: 18.00% charged on top of PayPal's fee (effective total deduction = Amount × 0.05192 + $0.354).
  • PayPal FX Conversion Margin: Typically 3.50%–4.00% below Google mid-market rate. If left empty, a logical median of 3.80% is automatically applied.
  • Indian 24h Auto-Withdrawal Hold: Under RBI regulations, PayPal India does not allow holding foreign currency wallets. Received USD is held up to 24 hours and automatically swept to your linked Indian bank. The conversion rate applied is determined at the exact time of auto-withdrawal.
  • Option 1 (Market Parity + 24h Buffer): Adds protection for PayPal's hidden currency spread plus a 1.25% overnight volatility buffer so you are guaranteed the full true market rupee value.
  • Option 2 (Standard Client Terms): Passes only the official 4.4% + $0.30 fee and GST to the client, leaving standard PayPal conversion intact to keep billing standard and client-friendly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PayPal fees and conversions

The PayPal Fee Conversion Calculator is a free tool that helps Indian freelancers, businesses, and service providers estimate exactly how much INR they will receive when a US-based client sends them a payment in USD through PayPal. It accounts for PayPal's transaction fee (4.4% + $0.30), Indian GST (18% on the fee), and PayPal's currency conversion markup (3.8% below market rate).

PayPal charges a standard international commercial transaction fee of 4.4% + $0.30 (fixed) for receiving payments in India. On top of this, the Indian government levies 18% GST on the PayPal fee. So if you receive $100, the PayPal fee is $4.70 (4.4% of $100 + $0.30), and GST on that fee is $0.85 (18% of $4.70), making total deductions $5.55 in USD.

PayPal does not use the real-time Google or market exchange rate for currency conversion. Instead, they apply a 3.8% markup below the mid-market rate. For example, if the current Google USD to INR rate is ₹85.00, PayPal would convert at approximately ₹81.77 (85.00 × 0.962). This hidden margin is one of the most significant costs of using PayPal for international transactions.

Unfortunately, for Indian PayPal accounts, you cannot hold a USD balance — PayPal automatically converts the received amount to INR at their exchange rate. The only way to reduce this cost is to use alternative payment platforms like Wise (TransferWise), Payoneer, or direct bank wire transfers, which typically offer better exchange rates with lower markups.

This calculator provides a very close estimate based on the current live exchange rate and PayPal's publicly stated fee structure. However, actual amounts may vary slightly because PayPal's internal exchange rate fluctuates in real-time and may differ from the mid-market rate at the exact moment of conversion. We recommend using this as a planning tool rather than an exact predictor.

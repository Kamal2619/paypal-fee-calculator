Frequently Asked Questions

A free tool that helps Indian freelancers estimate exactly how much INR they'll receive from a USD PayPal payment. It accounts for the 4.4% + $0.30 fee, 18% GST, and PayPal's 3.8% FX markup.

PayPal charges 4.4% + $0.30 for international receipts in India. The Indian government adds 18% GST on that fee. For $100: fee = $4.70, GST = $0.85, total deduction = $5.55.

PayPal uses a rate 3.8% lower than the mid-market rate. If Google shows ₹85/USD, PayPal converts at ~₹81.77. This hidden margin is the biggest cost of using PayPal.

Indian PayPal accounts can't hold USD — auto-conversion is mandatory. Alternatives like Wise or Payoneer offer better rates with lower markups.
